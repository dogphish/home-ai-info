Use & support

Updated 30 October 2026

Private use

Home AI Family is a self-hosted organiser for a household. This public website provides information about it; it does not offer public accounts, a paid subscription or access to a household’s app.

Connected services

Only connect accounts you are authorised to use. Google connections are optional and can be disconnected. Google’s own terms and account settings continue to apply. Home AI Family is an independent project and is not endorsed by Google or GitHub.

Review before acting

Check important dates and details against their source. Email content and locally generated suggestions may be incomplete or incorrect. Personal calendar additions require explicit confirmation. Advertised prices can change and may exclude charges or conditions; verify the full offer at the seller before deciding to buy. Home does not book or purchase on your behalf.

Support and privacy

The household administrator manages access, availability, updates and backups. Contact them privately for account or data questions. General software issues can be raised in the public information repository, without posting personal data or credentials.

Read the privacy policy for Google permissions, local processing, retention and deletion.
