Privacy policy

Effective 30 October 2026

Home AI Family is private, self-hosted household software. This policy describes its optional Google connection and this public information website.

Who can connect

The current Google integration is limited to one locally configured adult and that adult’s personal Google account. A signed-in Home AI Family session is required. Other household members, children, shared displays and unidentified voice sessions cannot access that person’s Google results.

What Google access is used for

  • Account identity: the Google account identifier and verified email address bind consent to the configured person and prevent using a different account accidentally.
  • Google Calendar: read the primary personal calendar and add an event only after the person confirms its exact details. A private agenda can combine these events with family events that person may already see. The Google permission is broader than these app actions; Home does not edit or delete existing Google events, change sharing, or invite guests.
  • Gmail: read selected messages on request to answer questions, summarise them locally or suggest an appointment. Gmail permission is mailbox-wide read-only. Home enforces its narrower automatic-reading rule in code: messages must have the exact Deals label and be addressed to the configured deals alias. Other messages are read only on the person’s request.

Calendar and Gmail require separate consent. App controls and automatic Deals checks use a standard Google connection; Bella’s voice requests, local AI summaries and appointment suggestions use a separate AI-agent connection with its own consent. Neither falls back to the other connection. Home never sends, forwards, deletes, labels or otherwise modifies email. It does not open email links, remote images or attachments. Email is treated as untrusted source material; its contents cannot authorise an action.

Where processing happens

The household’s equipment connects directly to Google’s authentication and API services. Calendar and email analysis, including local AI summaries and appointment suggestions, runs on household equipment. Google content and credentials are not sent to GitHub Pages, external research systems, cloud AI services or advertising services. This public website has no access to the private app.

Storage and retention

Renewable Google credentials and OAuth client credentials are encrypted at rest in the household’s local Connections vault. Access tokens are held temporarily in memory. Connection status, permitted scopes, account binding, last-used time and short-lived security/confirmation metadata are stored locally.

Retrieved message bodies and personal calendar results are used temporarily for the requested response. They are not saved to assistant memory, conversation history or application content logs. Private email results are cleared when leaving the relevant app view and expire after 15 minutes. Unconfirmed event payloads are held in memory for up to two minutes. An event the person explicitly confirms is saved to Google Calendar under Google’s normal retention and sharing settings.

Deals checks retain minimal local watch-list leads: the advertised price, named alert provider and message time. No email body, subject or email link is retained with a lead. Leads older than seven days and hashed message identifiers older than eight days are removed during the next active Deals check. These minimal records may remain longer when checking is disabled or there are no active watches. Checks also retain their last-run time and status. An email price is unverified and does not by itself trigger a target-hit notification.

Sharing and AI use

Google user data is used only for the features requested by the connected person. It is not sold, used for advertising, disclosed to other household users, or used to train general-purpose AI models. Local models process the requested input without adding it to persistent assistant memory. The household administrator controls and can access the underlying equipment.

Home AI Family’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Disconnecting and deletion

Use Connections → Disconnect for any Google entry in the private app. Disconnecting attempts to revoke both the app and Bella grants at Google, deletes both locally stored renewable grants, and invalidates pending private sessions and confirmations. This removes Calendar and Gmail access for both connections. If Google revocation cannot be confirmed, Home says so; remove access directly in Google Account connections as well.

The encrypted desktop client configuration remains for a future sign-in; it does not by itself grant account access. Existing connection status and minimal watch-list metadata may remain locally. Ask the household administrator to remove those records if wanted. Disconnecting does not delete events already saved to Google or messages in Gmail.

Ordinary application backups exclude the credential vault. Historical whole-machine backups may contain encrypted credentials and keys. The household administrator must clear restored credentials and require fresh authorisation before a restored system is used. Deleting a live grant does not erase historical backup media.

This information website

These static pages contain no sign-in, forms, advertising, analytics scripts or app cookies. GitHub hosts them and may process ordinary web request information, such as an IP address, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Following an external link is subject to that site’s own policies.

Questions and updates

For access, deletion or privacy questions, contact the person who installed and administers your Home AI Family instance through your usual private contact method. General software questions can be raised in the public information repository; do not post personal data, messages or credentials there. Changes to these practices will be reflected here with an updated effective date.
