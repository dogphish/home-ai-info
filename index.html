Plans, reminders and useful information, in one place at home. Home AI Family is a self-hosted app for a household, with optional connections controlled by the person who uses them.

How your Google data is handled ↗
01

Make room for plans

A family calendar brings household plans and reminders together. An authorised adult can also read their personal Google Calendar and explicitly confirm a new personal event.

02

Find what matters

With separate Gmail consent, the connected adult can ask about recent messages or an appointment. Gmail access is read-only. Suggested calendar additions always need confirmation.

03

Keep an eye on deals

Automatic email checks are limited to the exact Deals label and the configured deals address. Relevant advertised prices become private watch-list leads for checking.

Personal means personal

Google results are available only in the connected adult’s authenticated session. They are processed on household equipment and are kept out of children’s views, shared screens, external research and cloud AI services.

This is the public information site. The household app is hosted separately on a private network, with no public sign-up here.
